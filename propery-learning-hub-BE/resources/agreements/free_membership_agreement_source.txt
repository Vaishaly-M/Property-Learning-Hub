BACKGROUND
The Academy provides training, mentoring, tools and resources relating to Serviced Accommodation (“SA”) business models in the United Kingdom. From time to time the Academy runs promotions under which it waives its Standard Fee (as defined below) and offers free membership access to the Programme to selected individuals, in exchange for those individuals providing the Academy with the Marketing Consideration described in this Agreement. This Agreement applies to the Client's enrolment under the Academy's promotion identified as: [insert promotion name/date].
The Parties agree as follows:
1. Definitions and Interpretation
1.1 “Commencement Date” means the date on which the Client is granted access to the Online Portal, or the date of the 1:1 Strategy Day, whichever is earlier.
1.2 “Programme Materials” means all training content, templates, recordings, documents, and resources made available via the Online Portal or otherwise.
1.3 “Platform Software” means the third-party or Academy-provided software tools referenced in clause 3, including Infinit Invoice, Infinit Doc, Guestalizer, the CRM, and the PMS.
1.4 “First Deal” means the Client's first legally completed Serviced Accommodation agreement (e.g. a signed rent-to-SA, management, or lease agreement) sourced with the assistance of the Programme.
1.5 “Standard Fee” means £3,000, being the Academy's standard enrolment fee for the Programme where a client enrols on a paid basis, referenced in clauses 5, 7, and 10.
1.6 “Marketing Consideration” means, together, the rights granted under clause 9.1, the Testimonials described in clause 9.2, and the Active Participation requirements in clause 9.5.
1.7 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

• Access to the Deal Analyser tool;
• Access to a private group chat for Academy members;
• Access to Infinit Invoice (invoicing software);
• Access to Infinit Doc (document signing software);
• Access to a Client Relationship Management (CRM) system;
• Access to a Property Management System (PMS);
• Access to Guestalizer;
• Access to template property-related contracts and documents;
• Access to a bookings platform; and
• Access to a deals marketplace,
(together, “the Services”), each on a non-exclusive, non-transferable basis for the duration of the Term (clause 4).

5.3 For the avoidance of doubt, nothing in this Agreement obliges the Client to make any payment to the Academy unless, and only to the extent that, clause 10 applies.

The Academy will not edit the Testimonials in a way that materially misrepresents the Client's genuine views.
9.5 The Client will: (a) attend at least the minimum percentage of scheduled weekly meetings set out in Schedule 1, during the Client's support period under clause 4; and (b) remain an active member of the Academy's group chat, and not leave, mute, or otherwise cease engaging with the group chat for longer than the maximum period set out in Schedule 1, without reasonable explanation (“Active Participation”).
10. Consequences of Non-Provision of the Marketing Consideration
10.1 If the Client fails to provide any part of the Marketing Consideration in accordance with clause 9 and Schedule 1, the Academy will give the Client written notice specifying the failure and allowing the Client no less than 14 days to remedy it.
10.2 If the Client has not remedied the failure within that period, the Academy may, by further written notice to the Client, treat the fee waiver in clause 5.2 as withdrawn with effect from the date of that notice, and invoice the Client for the Standard Fee, payable within 30 days of the invoice.
10.3 Clause 10.2 reflects the value of the Services the Client has already received in circumstances where the Client has not provided the Marketing Consideration agreed in exchange for them; it is not a penalty for breach. The Academy will exercise this right reasonably and proportionately, taking into account the extent of the Client's non-compliance and any circumstances the Client has raised with the Academy.
10.4 The Academy will not issue an invoice under clause 10.2 where the Client's failure to provide the Marketing Consideration arises from circumstances reasonably outside the Client's control (for example, ill health, bereavement, or other serious personal circumstances), provided the Client notifies the Academy of those circumstances as soon as reasonably possible.
10.5 Failure to pay an invoice issued under clause 10.2 within the period stated is a material breach of this Agreement, entitling the Academy to suspend or terminate the Client's access to the Services under clause 11.
11. Termination
11.1 Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other Party commits a material breach of this Agreement which (if capable of remedy) is not remedied within 14 days of written notice.
11.2 The Academy may suspend or terminate the Client's access to the Services immediately if the Client breaches clause 8, engages in abusive conduct towards Academy staff or other members, or uses the Services for any unlawful purpose.
11.3 On termination for any reason, the Client's licence to use the Programme Materials and Platform Software ends immediately. Clauses 1, 5, 7, 8, 9, 10, 12, and 13.4 to 13.6 shall survive termination, together with any other provision which by its nature is intended to survive termination.
