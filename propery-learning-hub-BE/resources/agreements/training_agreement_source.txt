BACKGROUND
The Academy provides training, mentoring, tools and resources relating to Serviced Accommodation (“SA”) business models in the United Kingdom, to help students understand the fundamentals of SA and develop their own property businesses. The Client wishes to enrol on the Academy's training and membership programme (“the Programme”) on the terms of this Agreement.

1.3 “Platform Software” means the third-party or Academy-provided software tools referenced in clause 3, including Infinit Invoice, Infinit Doc, Guestalizer, Deal Analizer, Bookalizer, the CRM, and the PMS.
1.4 “First Deal” means the Client's first legally completed Serviced Accommodation agreement (e.g. a signed rent-to-SA, management, or lease agreement) sourced with the assistance of the Programme.
